Frequently Asked Questions

What are SSC photo and signature specifications?

SSC requires a photograph of 3.5 cm width × 4.5 cm height (20KB - 50KB) and signature of 4.0 cm width × 2.0 cm height (10KB - 20KB) in JPEG format.

Will SSC reject photo without date?

SSC latest guidelines state that the photo must be clear, without cap or spectacles, taken with a plain white or light background. Date on photo is optional unless specified in the latest notification.
