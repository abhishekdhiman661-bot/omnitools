Frequently Asked Questions

What is the formula to remove (reverse) GST?

Formula: Original Amount = Total Amount × 100 ÷ (100 + GST Rate). For example, if total is ₹1,180 with 18% GST, Base Amount = ₹1,000 and GST = ₹180.

How are CGST and SGST divided?

In intra-state sales, GST is equally split 50-50 between Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). For 18% GST, CGST is 9% and SGST is 9%.
