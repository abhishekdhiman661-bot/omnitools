About OmniTools

OmniTools was founded with a straightforward mission: make everyday digital utilities fast, free, and genuinely private for everyone worldwide.

The Problem We Solve

Most online file converters and photo resizers force users to upload personal photographs, identity documents, and sensitive financial records to remote cloud servers. This exposes users to server downtime, queue delays, and severe privacy risks.

Our 100% Client-Side Architecture

Every tool on OmniTools runs entirely within your device's browser memory using modern web standards:

  • HTML5 Canvas & WebAssembly: Images are compressed and resized pixel-by-pixel right on your phone or PC.
  • PDF-Lib: PDF documents are merged and organized locally without transferring a single byte over the web.
  • Zero Data Storage: Because we don't operate file storage backends, your data cannot be leaked, sold, or snooped upon.
