Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

1. Our Zero-Upload Principle

At OmniTools, user privacy is not an afterthought; it is built into our core architecture. We do not upload, transmit, process, or store your photos, PDFs, text, or calculations on any remote server.

2. In-Browser Client-Side Processing

When you select a passport photograph, compress an image, calculate your GST, or merge PDF files, all computations occur exclusively within your web browser's local sandbox memory using client-side JavaScript, WebAssembly, and HTML5 Canvas API. When you close the browser tab, all temporary data is instantly cleared from memory.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

OmniTools uses minimal local browser storage (`localStorage`) solely to remember your chosen user interface preferences (such as Dark Mode or Light Mode). We do not use intrusive tracking cookies or fingerprinting techniques.

4. Third-Party Advertising Partners

We may display non-intrusive advertisements (such as Google AdSense) to support the ongoing development of these free tools. Third-party vendors may use standard cookies to serve relevant advertisements based on prior visits to websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated modification date.
